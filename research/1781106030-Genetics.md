## 1. Follow-up Questions

1. Which published RCTs of PRS feedback explicitly report follow-up intervention access as a controlled or stratified variable, and what are their reported effect sizes?
2. In trials that equalize clinical follow-up (e.g., universal screening access post-feedback), does PRS feedback move behavior beyond provision of equivalent general risk counseling?
3. What is the typical effect size (OR or risk difference) for PRS feedback on uptake of downstream screening or prevention behaviors, and how does it compare to non-genetic risk communication (e.g., family history, Framingham score)?
4. Do existing meta-analyses (e.g., Hollands et al.) distinguish between SES-confounded real-world effects and mechanistic "pure information" effects?
5. Could a sandbox simulation using published baseline rates, ORs, and SES-stratified uptake data reproduce or challenge the null findings reported in the literature?
