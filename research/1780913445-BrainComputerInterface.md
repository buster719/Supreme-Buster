**1. Follow-up questions**  
- Can we disentangle trust changes due to *perceived* neural readout (illusion of mind-reading) from actual readout fidelity using sham BCI conditions?  
- Does dynamic transparency about what the AI *doubts* or *ignores* in the neural signal reduce over-trust more effectively than static explanations?  
- How does trust evolve when the AI uses neural signals to *challenge* the user’s self-report (e.g., “you look more stressed than you say”) compared to when it silently adapts?  
- In longitudinal use, does habituation to BCI-mediated adaptation cause a trust drop when the AI later operates without neural access (withdrawal effect)?  
- Which individual difference measures (e.g., neuroticism, privacy concern, tech self-efficacy) best predict vulnerability to BCI-driven trust miscalibration?
