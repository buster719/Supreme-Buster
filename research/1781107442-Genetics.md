## 1. Follow-up Questions

1. What is the gap between SNP-heritability estimates (e.g., from GCTA-GREML) and variance actually captured by polygenic risk scores (PRS) in out-of-sample prediction, stratified by trait and sample size?
2. How does population structure (ancestry mismatch between training and test cohorts) degrade discriminatory AUC in real datasets, and what fraction of the gap is attributable to LD architecture differences vs. effect-size portability?
3. What have simulation studies with known architectures (e.g., α-mixture of effect sizes, explicit epistasis) shown about the recoverable R² under realistic sample sizes (N < 500k)?
4. How much does incorporating gene–gene interaction (epistasis) or gene–environment interaction models improve discriminatory power over additive PRS in practice?
5. What do CAGI (Critical Assessment of Genome Interpretation) challenge rounds reveal about the ceiling of variant-effect prediction accuracy for clinically relevant phenotypes?
