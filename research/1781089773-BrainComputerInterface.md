## 1. Follow-Up Questions

1. Do individuals high in trait neuroticism show steeper trust erosion curves during repeated BCI calibration failures compared to low-neuroticism users?
2. Does prior "BCI literacy" (understanding of signal noise, training requirements, stochastic decoding) moderate the negative effect of challenge frequency on trust?
3. Can trait anxiety be distinguished from state anxiety in BCI performance contexts, and do they predict trust trajectories differently?
4. Do conscientiousness and trait self-control (separate from neuroticism) predict persistence and trust recovery after BCI-induced frustration?
5. What is the minimal viable "challenge dosage" that builds resilience without triggering avoidance behavior across personality profiles?
