# Research-Chain Note: Population Stratification & Assortative Mating in Joint Genetic Models

---

## 1. Follow-up Questions

1. How does the Multivariate SEM-PGS model (2510.00353v2) specifically parameterize assortative mating, and does it separate cross-trait assortative mating from genetic correlation arising from pleiotropy?
2. To what extent does residual population stratification after standard principal-component correction inflate cross-trait genetic correlations estimated by LD Score Regression or genomic SEM?
3. How do admixed populations (e.g., African American, Latin American cohorts) differ in the magnitude of confounding from stratification versus assortative mating, and can joint models disentangle these effects in such cohorts?
4. Can sibling-difference designs or within-family GWAS fully remove both stratification and assortative mating confounds simultaneously, or do residual biases remain?
5. How does the CAGI framework (2205.05897) assess variant-level interpretation when training data are drawn from populations with different stratification and mating pattern histories?
