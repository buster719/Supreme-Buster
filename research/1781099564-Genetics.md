**Research-chain note: NHMRC Mitochondrial Donation Eligibility Criteria**

**1. Follow-up questions**
- What specific legislative or regulatory documents does the NHMRC use to authorize mitochondrial donation under the Mitochondrial Donation Law Reform (Maeve’s Law) 2022?
- Does the NHMRC’s Mitochondrial Donation Advisory Committee (MDAC) publish clinical guidelines that list approved conditions or use a broader threshold-based assessment?
- Are there publicly accessible registers of licensed mitochondrial donation treatments or approved clinics in Australia that list the conditions treated?
- How does the Australian eligibility process differ from the UK HFEA’s approach of maintaining a list of specific approved conditions?
- What role does the Australasian CRE (Centre for Research Excellence) in mitochondrial disease play in developing or recommending eligibility criteria?

**2. Verification leads**
- **Primary source**: Australian *Mitochondrial Donation Law Reform (Maeve’s Law) 2022* – the legislation itself and its explanatory memorandum. Available on Australian Parliament website.
- **Primary source**: NHMRC’s official guidelines for mitochondrial donation – look for “NHMRC Mitochondrial Donation Guidance” or similar. NHMRC publishes clinical and ethical guidelines.
- **Primary source**: Terms of reference and meeting minutes/publications of the NHMRC Mitochondrial Donation Advisory Committee (MDAC). Check NHMRC website or Australian Government department sites.
- **Secondary verification**: Search for “NHMRC mitochondrial donation eligible conditions list” or “NHMRC mitochondrial donation clinical criteria”. Also check recent news releases from NHMRC (e.g., first approved treatments).
- **Academic verification**: Search PubMed/Australian medical journals for reviews discussing Maeve’s Law implementation, especially by clinicians involved in the process (e.g., from Melbourne IVF, Royal Women’s Hospital).
- **Uncertain lead**: The actual clinical application forms or pro forma used by licensed clinics may detail eligibility criteria, but these may not be publicly available.
