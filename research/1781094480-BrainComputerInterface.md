# BCI Intelligence Measurement: Ecological Validity vs. Raw Performance

## 1. Follow-up questions
- What are the empirically measured information transfer rates (ITR) of modern passive/idle-state BCI paradigms compared to active evoked-potential (e.g., P300) or motor-imagery systems in both controlled and ecological settings?
- How do studies operationally define and measure "situational awareness" or "autonomous intelligence" in the context of BCI performance, and is there a validated metric that decouples this from raw ITR?
- Does longitudinal training with spontaneous/idle-state BCIs lead to improved signal-to-noise ratios that narrow the performance gap with evoked-signal BCIs, and does this effect transfer to novel ecological contexts?
- What specific experimental designs have successfully measured BCI performance in true "free behavior" paradigms, and what were their reported ITRs compared to lab benchmarks?
- Are there theoretical models or frameworks that formally define an "intelligence axis" for BCI systems, incorporating metrics like adaptability, context awareness, and user autonomy alongside ITR?
