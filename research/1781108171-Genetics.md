### 1. Follow-up Questions
- What specific statistical methods (e.g., rank correlation, absolute score accuracy) are used to evaluate PGT-P reliability across embryo rankings?
- Are there published datasets or studies that track clinical outcomes (e.g., live birth, euploidy confirmation) for embryos stratified by PGT-P rank within sibling cohorts?
- How do different PGT-P providers (e.g., MyOme, Orchid, Lifeview) report on accuracy variations across the score distribution, and do they disclose limitations for middle-ranked embryos?
- Could the "reliability" variation be an artifact of parental genetic background or assay technical noise rather than genuine biological signal?
