**1. Follow‑up questions**

- How does the *type* of BCI signal (evoked vs. spontaneous, discrete vs. continuous) affect the shape of the bandwidth‑intelligence tradeoff, not just the bitrate?
- In shared‑autonomy frameworks that learn a user’s intent over time, does the effective bandwidth *increase* as the machine model becomes more personalised, making direct comparison across bandwidth levels insufficient?
- Are there empirical studies that *independently* varied BCI information transfer rate and autonomous‑decision‑making authority level while holding task difficulty constant, and measured team completion speed / errors / user workload?
- When machine intelligence fails subtly (e.g. overconfident misrecognition of intent), does higher BCI bandwidth act as a safety buffer, or does it instead mask system faults that would otherwise trigger user correction?
- What is the minimum viable BCI bandwidth below which adding machine intelligence yields diminishing returns because the human cannot convey enough context for the AI to act appropriately?
