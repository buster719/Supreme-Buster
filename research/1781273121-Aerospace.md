**Research-chain note: Cumulative SEE and TID scaling in spacecraft interface controllers**

**1. Follow-up questions:**
- What specific failure modes (e.g., SEU in configuration memory, SET in combinatorial logic) dominate FPGA/ASIC recovery circuits under combined TID degradation and SEE bursts?
- How do commercial radiation-hardened (RHBD) FPGA families (e.g., Xilinx Versal, Microchip RTG4) quantify SEE rates *after* TID degradation in their datasheets or test reports?
- Are there empirical models (e.g., from NASA Goddard or ESA) that combine TID dose rates (~0.5 mrad(Si)/day in interplanetary cruise) with SEE cross-section curves to predict cumulative recovery-failure probability over a 6-month transit?
- What redundancy strategies (TMR, scrubbing, checkpointing) are most sensitive to the *interaction* of TID-induced threshold voltage shifts and SEE-induced transient glitches?
- Have any Mars transit missions (e.g., Curiosity, Perseverance) published logged data on single-event upsets in their controller FPGAs that could be correlated with TID estimates?

**2. Verification leads:**
- **Authoritative datasets:** NASA’s Space Radiation Analysis Group (SRAG) maintains mission-specific TID and SEE predictions. Check their RAD tool outputs for a 6-month Mars transit profile.
- **Key papers:** 
  - *IEEE Transactions on Nuclear Science* papers on “TID effects on FPGA configuration memory” (search for “TID SEE FPGA Mars” in IEEE Xplore).
  - ESA’s “Radiation Hardness Assurance” guidelines (ECSS-E-HB-10-12A) include SEE/TID interaction models.
  - NASA/JPL technical reports on Mars Science Laboratory radiation environment (e.g., JPL Publication 19-5).
- **Test data:** Vendor test reports for RHBD FPGAs often include “TID-SEE” correlation plots (e.g., Microchip’s RT PolarFire report).
