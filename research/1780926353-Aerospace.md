## 1. Follow-up questions
- Which specific mission failure modes in deep-space logistics show the largest drop in probability when fleet size increases from 1 to ≥10 per synodic window—propulsion, ECLSS, navigation, comms, or surface ops?
- To what extent does a shared orbital propellant depot (e.g. water from ISRU) create a non-linear reduction in total ΔV requirements for a fleet of 10 identical missions compared to each carrying its own fuel?
- How do the resilience gains from a redundant inter-spacecraft communication relay network scale with node count, and at what node density do diminishing returns appear?
- Does a standardized docking/berthing interface for Mars-bound payloads show a steeper resilience payoff curve than mission‑unique adapters when scaled to 10 simultaneous flights?
- What is the quantified survivability benefit of a jointly maintained in‑transit emergency spare parts cache across a 10‑mission fleet versus single‑mission self‑sufficiency?
