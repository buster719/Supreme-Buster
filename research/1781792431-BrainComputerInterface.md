## 1. Follow-up Questions

1. In Lee & See's original framework, trust recovery asymmetry is driven by cognitive/affective heuristics (negativity bias, slow rebuilding of mental models). Does the same mechanism operate when the "automation" is a neural decoder that physically reshapes the user's cortical representation over weeks of co-adaptation?
2. Does the time constant of neural strategy adaptation (order of days to weeks) create a second, slower recovery layer on top of the standard cognitive trust-recovery lag, and can these two layers be empirically separated
