# Research-Chain Note: B₂O₃ Viscosity and Crack Penetration in ZrB₂/HfB₂ at 1200–1600°C

## 1. Follow-up Questions

1. What is the measured B₂O₃ viscosity as a function of temperature in the 1000–1600°C range, and how sensitive is it to dissolved SiO₂ or other oxide impurities from the composite matrix?
2. What is the B₂O₃ evaporation rate (mass loss per unit area per time) as a function of temperature and ambient humidity/flow conditions?
3. Can capillary-driven penetration into a 5–10 µm crack be completed within the B₂O₃ residence time before depletion, given realistic crack geometries and oxide film thicknesses?
4. What role does ZrO₂/HfO₂ formation kinetics play—does the oxide shell close the crack before B₂O₃ can penetrate, or does it seal from the outside in?
5. Is there experimental microstructural evidence (SEM/TEM) of B₂O₃ glass found inside healed cracks in ZrB₂-SiC composites, and at what depth?
