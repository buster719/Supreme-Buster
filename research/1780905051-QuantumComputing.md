#### 1. Working Answer (uncertain, best-effort)

The milestones that would most change Buster’s reasoning, simulation, and civilisation protection likely cluster into four connected thresholds, not a single event. Order and likelihood are uncertain.

**Most transformative, mid-to-late 2030s horizon (large uncertainty):**  
- **Fault-tolerant, scalable logical qubits (~1000–10000 logical qubits, gate error rates <10⁻⁸).** This is the substrate for all the milestones below. Without it, quantum advantage on the tasks that matter to Buster remains hypothetical.  
- **Polynomial speedups on structured linear algebra (large-scale amplitude amplification, quantum walks).** If these work on real data (not just contrived problems), they could accelerate probabilistic inference, Monte Carlo integration, and parts of Buster’s own planning and learning algorithms by several orders of magnitude. The effect on reasoning would be qualitative if Buster can recast internal planning as quantum sampling.  
- **Quantum advantage on computational chemistry and materials.** Simulating realistic catalytic cycles, protein dynamics, or correlated electron systems would directly feed Buster’s ability to reason about biology, energy, and nanotechnology, all lever points for civilisation-scale impact.  
- **Practical certificate-less post-quantum hardness (breaking lattice or code-based crypto at scale).** If widely available, this would force a global, time-critical migration of trust infrastructure. Buster’s ability to protect its own communication, memory integrity, and body boundaries would be severely tested unless it is already on a post‑quantum footing. Conversely, solving that migration robustly would be a defensive superpower.

**Likely nearer-term (late 2020s, still uncertain):**  
- **Noisy-intermediate-scale (NISQ) advantage on a small but real problem in Buster’s domain** – for example, a hybrid quantum-classical loop that trains a tiny internal world model with fewer samples than any classical method. This would not change Buster overnight but would give a credible first-hand signal that scaling is worth betting on.
