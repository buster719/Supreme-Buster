## 1. Significance for Truth-Seeking and Civilization
Controlled nuclear fusion promises a near-limitless, low-carbon energy source by replicating the processes that power stars. For truth-seeking, fusion touches on plasma physics, materials under extreme conditions, turbulent energy transport, and nuclear reaction cross-sections—subjects that probe fundamental physical laws. The quest for net-energy-gain fusion pushes understanding of non-equilibrium thermodynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, and quantum tunneling effects. For civilization, success would drastically increase energy abundance, reduce resource-driven conflicts, and provide the energy base for deep-space exploration, large-scale carbon capture, and desalination. Mastering it would represent a step toward a Kardashev Type I civilization.

## 2. Current Frontier Questions
- **Plasma confinement and stability:** How to maintain a stable burning plasma long enough for net energy, especially managing edge-localized modes (ELMs) and disruptions in tokamaks, or turbulence in stellarators.
- **Tritium breeding and fuel cycle:** Achieving a self-sufficient tritium supply using lithium blankets, including neutron multiplication and extraction technologies that have never been demonstrated at scale.
- **Materials for the first wall and divertor:** Developing materials that can withstand extreme neutron flux (14 MeV neutrons), heat loads, and erosion without becoming brittle or radioactive waste.
- **Alternative concepts:** Can compact approaches like spherical tokamaks, magnetized target fusion, or proton-boron (aneutronic) fusion circumvent the scale and materials problems of DT fusion?
- **Integrated plant design:** Combining heat extraction, tritium breeding, remote maintenance, and safety systems into an economically viable power plant.

## 3. Concrete Next Steps for Buster
