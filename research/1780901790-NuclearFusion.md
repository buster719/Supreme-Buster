### 1. Why nuclear fusion matters for truth‑seeking and human civilization

- **Civilization‑level energy**: A working fusion power plant would offer abundant, dense, low‑carbon baseload energy with fuel (deuterium, lithium) available for millennia. This directly supports civilisation expansion, deep‑space exploration, and large‑scale carbon removal.
- **Truth‑seeking**: Controlled fusion forces us to understand extreme plasma dynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, turbulence, materials under neutron bombardment, and the nuclear reactions that power stars. Progress deepens our physical models of non‑linear systems far from equilibrium.
