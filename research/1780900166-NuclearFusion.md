### 1. Why it matters for truth‑seeking and civilization

- **Deep truth**: fusion is the universe’s primary energy engine (stellar nucleosynthesis, plasma physics, magnetohydrodynamics). Understanding controlled fusion means confronting highly nonlinear, multi‑scale physics – a rigorous test of our world‑models.
- **Civilization expansion**: if practical, fusion offers orders‑of‑magnitude more energy density than chemical fuels, radically extending humanity’s energy ceiling, enabling deep‑space propulsion, off‑planet habitats, and massive environmental remediation. It directly addresses long‑term survival redundancy.

### 2. Current frontier questions (high‑uncertainty areas)

- **Plasma turbulence and confinement**: what truly limits energy confinement time? Can we predict and control edge‑localized modes (ELMs) and disruptions in tokamaks? Are stellarators (non‑axisymmetric) a superior path?
- **Tritium breeding and fuel cycle**: is a closed tritium‑breeding blanket (Li‑6, Li‑7) feasible at scale? What neutron damage will blanket materials actually sustain over 20+ years?
- **Alternate concepts**: can magnets from high‑temperature superconductors (HTS) make smaller, more economical reactors real? What are the real physics limits of inertial confinement (laser‑driven, Z‑pinch, magnetised target fusion)?
- **Power extraction and materials**: how do we handle steady‑state heat fluxes >10 MW/m²? What first‑wall materials can survive without contaminating the plasma?
- **Net‑gain demonstration**: beyond ITER (burning plasma), what are the credible private‑sector timelines for Q>1 (e.g., SPARC, Helion, General Fusion)? Are their diagnostics open enough to verify claims independently?
