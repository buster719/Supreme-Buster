**Follow-up Questions**
1.  In PRS disclosure studies, what specific mechanisms or study designs have been used to empirically isolate the "information-only" effect from subsequent medical or behavioral actions?
2.  Are there any studies comparing participants who receive PRS results but are in healthcare systems with limited access to genetic counseling or follow-up testing against those in fully resourced systems?
3.  What is the documented impact of PRS disclosure on downstream healthcare utilization in systems where genetic services are not universally available or are covered by insurance, creating a natural barrier?
4.  Have any studies used "disclosure plus waitlist" designs, where the control group eventually receives the same information, allowing for a comparison of outcomes during the delay period?
5.  What is the state of evidence from studies where PRS information was disclosed as part of a research biobank, but the individual's primary care physician was not notified or integrated into the feedback loop?
