# Research-Chain Note: Within-Family vs Population-Level PRS Effect Sizes

## 1. Follow-up Questions

1. What is the typical ratio of within-family PRS prediction accuracy to population-level PRS prediction accuracy across major disease categories (cardiovascular, metabolic, psychiatric, autoimmune)?
2. How much of the population-level PRS effect is attributable specifically to population stratification vs assortative mating vs indirect genetic effects (dynastic effects), and can these be cleanly decomposed?
3. Do within-family PRS effect sizes vary systematically by disease architecture (e.g., highly polygenic vs oligogenic conditions), and if so, what is the pattern?
4. How do sibling-based PRS predictions perform when the sibling pair is discordant for the disease, compared to unrelated individual predictions?
5. What empirical evidence exists on whether within-family PRS effect sizes are stable across ancestries, or whether ancestry-specific attenuation also occurs within families?

## 2. Verification Leads

- **Kong et al. (2018), Science**: "The nature of nurture: Effects of parental genotypes." Directly measures indirect genetic effects (dynastic effects) in Icelandic data using within-family designs. Verifiable via DOI.
- **Howe et al. (2022), Nature**: "Within-sibship genome-wide association analyses decrease bias in estimates of direct genetic effects." Large-scale within-sibship GWAS across UK Biobank + other cohorts. Reports attenuation of many population-level associations.
- **Young et al. (2018, 2019) PNAS/Science**: Papers on within-family GWAS and confounding by population structure and assortative mating. Estimates inflation factors.
- **Selzam et al. (2019), Behavior Genetics**: Compares family-based vs population-based PRS prediction for educational attainment and other traits. Reports significant attenuation.
- **Pingault et al. (2021), Nature Communications**: Within-family GWAS and PRS for multiple psychiatric traits showing substantial attenuation.
- **UK Biobank sibling pairs data**: Available for secondary analysis; contains thousands of sibling pairs genotyped and phenotyped.
- **Search query**: "within-family polygenic risk score attenuation site:nature.com OR site:science.org OR site:biorxiv.org" (uncertain: results may vary).
- **Lello et al. (2018), Scientific Reports**: "Accurate genomic prediction of human height" — discusses family-based prediction.
