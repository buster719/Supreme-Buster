**Research-chain note: PRS disclosure in research biobanks without physician integration**

**1. Follow-up questions:**
- What are the documented consent and return-of-results protocols in major research biobanks (e.g., UK Biobank, All of Us, MVP) that have collected genotyping/sequencing data and computed PRS?
- In studies where PRS results were returned to participants, was there any requirement or mechanism for informing the participant's primary care physician, or was the disclosure limited to the research team?
- What empirical studies have measured the psychological, behavioral, or clinical outcomes of returning PRS information directly to research participants without formal clinical follow-up?
- Are there published frameworks or case reports of adverse events where a participant learned a high PRS risk from a biobank study and sought clinical action outside the research setting, potentially causing harm or confusion?
- What governance documents or ethics reviews from biobank projects specifically address the issue of clinical integration or physician notification when returning genetic risk information?

**2. Verification leads:**
- **Direct evidence needed:** Search PubMed for: `("polygenic risk score" OR PRS) AND (return OR disclosure OR "return of results") AND (biobank OR cohort) AND ("primary care" OR physician OR "clinician notification" OR integration)`.
- **Key biobank protocols:** Review the *UK Biobank* Access Procedures and Material Transfer Agreement sections on "Return of Incidental Findings" and "Feedback to Participants." Also check *All of Us* Research Program's "Return of Results" policy documents.
- **Relevant ethics literature:** Look for empirical studies in journals like *Genetics in Medicine*, *Hastings Center Report*, or *Nature Reviews Genetics* on the ethics of returning polygenic scores in research settings.
- **Possible datasets:** The *ClinGen* Evidence Repository may have entries on PRS clinical utility, but may not address the biobank-specific feedback loop. The *CAGI* (Critical Assessment of Genome Interpretation) challenges might have data on how PRS are used in research contexts.
- **Uncertain:** Whether any biobank has published a formal analysis of participant actions after PRS disclosure
