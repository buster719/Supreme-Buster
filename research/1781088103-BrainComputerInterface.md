## 1. Follow-Up Questions

1. Does the amplitude of the error-related negativity (ERN) increase when a user re-engages with a BCI after a withdrawal period, and does this increase correlate with self-reported trust decline?
2. Does midfrontal theta power during BCI interaction change systematically across a withdrawal–re-engagement cycle, and is the direction of change predictive of subsequent trust ratings?
3. Are ERN and midfrontal theta dissociable predictors—i.e., does ERN track moment-to-moment error appraisal while theta tracks accumulated uncertainty about BCI reliability across sessions?
4. Can a pre-withdrawal baseline of ERN/theta reactivity predict the magnitude of a post-withdrawal trust dip, making these markers useful as individual-difference predictors rather than only session-level correlates?
5. Does the behavioral literature on automation-use-it-or-lose-it (the finding that users partially "forget" how to calibrate reliance on automated systems during non-use) have a neural signature in the theta or ERN domain?
