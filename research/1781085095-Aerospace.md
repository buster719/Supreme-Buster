### 1. Follow-up Questions

- Has anyone modeled stochastic closure (e.g., Markov-switching open/closed states) in the context of ISRU (In-Situ Resource Utilization) supply chains for lunar or Mars habitats specifically?
- Do NASA's CHAPEA or HERA analog missions produce publicly available supply-chain resilience simulation data?
- Are there agent-based models of autonomous habitat logistics under communication delay (4–24 min Earth-Mars) that incorporate supply interruption probability distributions?
- Is there a gap analysis comparing terrestrial supply chain resilience frameworks (NIST, ISO 22301) against closed-loop life support system requirements for deep space?
