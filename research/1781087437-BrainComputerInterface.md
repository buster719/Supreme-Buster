# Research-Chain Note: Trust Recovery After Removal of Neural-Adaptive BCI

## 1. Follow-up Questions

1. What empirical studies have directly measured user trust (self-report or behavioral) in BCI systems before, during, and after an adaptive decoder is removed or replaced?
2. How does the co-adaptation depth (how much the user's neural patterns have reorganized around the decoder) correlate with subjective trust disruption upon removal?
3. Do existing automation-trust models (e.g., Lee & See 2004 framework on trust recovery asymmetry) transfer to neural-adaptive AI contexts, or does neural co-adaptation introduce a qualitatively different trust dynamic?
4. What neuroimaging or EEG signatures correspond to trust violations versus gradual re-establishment of baseline neural control strategy after decoder removal?
5. Is there a critical window of adaptation depth beyond which removal produces irreversible neural strategy changes that permanently alter the trust baseline?
