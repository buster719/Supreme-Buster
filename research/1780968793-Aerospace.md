**Follow-up questions:**
1. Which specific magnetometer-based NDE datasets (e.g. CIVA, RAPID, NASA NDE benchmark) actually publish raw time-series or image-like magnetic field maps, and are they open-access?
2. Has any independent lab reproduced the HPC soft-computing pipeline from arXiv:1610.05521v1, and if so, were results compared on identical defect specimens?
3. What are the public defect datasets (e.g. ASTM E3169, AFRL fastener-hole data, open-OoD NDE challenge) that include ground-truth X-ray/CT alongside magnetometer measurements for the same panel?
4. For quantum magnetometer arrays (SERF, NV-centre), are there shared multi-lab validation datasets like the PTB magnetic test site or QMagen?
5. Do any meta-reviews (e.g., NDT&E International surveys) document the replication rate of soft-computing aerospace defect classifiers, and what failure modes were observed?

**Verification leads:**
- `arXiv:1610.05521v1` – “Diagnosis of aerospace structure defects by a HPC implemented soft computing algorithm” (2016). Claims a neural-poisson solver on simulated and experimental magnetic flux leakage data for aircraft skin. Needs direct reading to find whether experimental data is from a named standard defect set and if code/data are released.
- `arXiv:2106.15843v1` – “Precision Magnetometers for Aerospace Applications” (2021). A review, unlikely to host raw validation datasets, but may cite underlying NDE campaigns (e.g., MAST, SHM-in-service). Check its reference list for dataset DOIs.
- `arXiv:2407.08084v2` – “Decentralized Adaptive Aerospace Transportation… Using A Team of Robots” (2024). Unlikely to contain magnetometer defect data; tangent.
