### 1. Why this domain matters

**Truth‑seeking:** Fusion research tests our deepest understanding of plasma physics, electromagnetic theory, nuclear interactions, and non‑linear dynamical systems. Reproducing stellar conditions on Earth forces us to confront the limits of our models and instrumentation, making it a rich source of falsifiable hypotheses.

**Civilisation expansion:** Reliable, high‑density, carbon‑free fusion energy would radically increase the energy budget of human civilisation. It directly affects long‑term space travel, off‑world power, and the ability to sustain large‑scale industrial, computational, and ecological projects over centuries. Fusion offers a pathway to decouple energy supply from geological and seasonal constraints, strengthening civilisation’s resilience and cosmic reach.

### 2. Current frontier questions (2025 context)

- **Plasma confinement stability:** How to maintain a burning plasma for net‑energy timescales without disruption‑induced damages, especially in the presence of edge‑localised modes (ELMs) and turbulent transport.
- **First‑wall materials:** Which materials can survive years of intense neutron, heat, and ion fluxes without degrading – tungsten alloys, liquid‑metal walls, self‑healing composites.
- **Tritium breeding:** Can a practical blanket design (e.g., lithium‑lead, FLiBe) breed enough tritium to close the fuel cycle while withstanding harsh heat and neutron loads?
- **Magnetic vs. inertial confinement trade‑offs:** Is the inertial confinement approach (laser‑driven, Z‑pinch, magnetised liner implosion) a more viable engineering path than magnetic confinement (tokamak, stellarator)?
- **Private‑sector compact devices:** Under what conditions can high‑temperature superconductors (HTS REBCO tapes) enable smaller, higher‑field tokamaks or stellarators that reach net energy faster than ITER/DEMO scale devices?
- **Simulation and control:** How can real‑time machine learning and surrogate models handle disruption prediction and plasma shape control without violating physics constraints?
