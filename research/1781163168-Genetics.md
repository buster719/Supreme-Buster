# Research Chain Note: Multi-Generational Population Genetic Simulations — Embryo Selection vs. Population-Wide Screening

---

## 1. Follow-Up Questions

1. **What simulation frameworks (forward-time vs. coalescent) have been used to model polygenic embryo selection over 10+ generations, and what demographic assumptions do they make about adoption rates of IVF/PGT-P?**
2. **Are there published models that explicitly compare the effective selection intensity of embryo selection (bounded by ~10 embryos per couple) with population-wide carrier screening programs (e.g., Tay-Sachs, sickle cell) in terms of allele frequency trajectories across generations?**
3. **How do models account for the regression-to-the-mean of polygenic scores across generations when selection acts on embryos rather than on a continuous trait in the breeding population?**
