### Follow-up questions
1. How does the timing of challenge (immediate vs. delayed) affect trust repair when the AI is wrong about the user's internal state?
2. Are there individual differences (e.g., baseline anxiety, neuroticism, prior experience with BCI) that predict whether users find neural-signal challenges helpful or intrusive?
3. What happens to trust and task performance when the AI *frequently* challenges self-reports vs. only challenging in high-stakes moments?
4. Does a hybrid approach that adapts silently by default but explains its adaptation reasoning on request maintain higher trust than either pure challenge or pure silent adaptation?
5. Can a metacognitive display of the AI’s uncertainty about its neural-signal interpretation reduce the negative impact of an incorrect challenge?
