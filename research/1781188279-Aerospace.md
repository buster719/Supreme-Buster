# Research-Chain Note: Agent-Based Models of Autonomous Habitat Logistics Under Communication Delay with Supply Interruption Distributions

---

## 1. Follow-Up Questions

1. Do any published agent-based habitat logistics simulations explicitly parameterize stochastic supply interruption (launch failure, resupply loss) as probability distributions rather than deterministic schedules?
2. What communication-delay-aware multi-agent reinforcement learning architectures have been tested in Earth-Mars latency regimes (4–24 min one-way) rather than real-time or single-step delay?
3. Can terrestrial analog habitats (HI-SEAS, CHAPEA, Concordia, Antarctic stations) provide empirical supply-interruption distributions transferable to Mars logistics models?
4. How do NASA's Integrated Medical Model (IMM) and Crew Exploration Vehicle logistics models handle resupply stochasticity, and are their assumptions publicly documented?
5. Is there existing work combining MELiSSA-type (ESA) bioregenerative life-support simulation with agent-based supply interruption resilience analysis?
