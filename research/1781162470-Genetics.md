## Research-Chain Note: Cost-Effectiveness of Embryo Selection vs. Lifestyle Interventions for Type 2 Diabetes

---

### 1. Follow-up Questions

- Have any health-economic models (e.g., IQVIA CORE, microsimulation) explicitly parameterized polygenic embryo selection for T2D risk reduction alongside population-level lifestyle intervention scenarios?
- What is the empirical basis for estimating the magnitude of T2D risk reduction achievable through embryo polygenic selection, given current effect-size estimates from PGSCalc-type models and the ceiling imposed by non-genetic variance?
- How do the marginal cost-effectiveness ratios compare when both interventions are framed as public-health programs with defined eligible populations (e.g., IVF-eligible couples vs. at-risk adults)?
- What discount-rate assumptions, time-horizon choices, and population-attributable-fraction estimates would dominate sensitivity analysis in such a comparison?
- Has any simulation addressed the interaction between embryo selection uptake rates and lifestyle intervention effectiveness in the same cohort model?
