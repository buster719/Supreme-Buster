## Follow-up Questions
1.  How does the predictive power of a PRS for height in one ancestry group (e.g., European) translate to risk prediction accuracy in another group (e.g., East Asian), and what specific metrics quantify that performance drop?
2.  What are the concrete clinical or interventional "utility" outcomes measured in studies attempting to use PRS for conditions like coronary artery disease, and how do they compare to the variance explained?
3.  Can studies disentangle the contribution of genetic versus environmental "matching" (e.g., socioeconomic status) to the apparent predictive power of a PRS for educational attainment in a specific cohort?
4.  What is the range of estimates for the proportion of a PRS's statistical signal that is due to indirect genetic effects (dynastic effects, population stratification) versus direct biological causation?
5.  How do simulation studies model the theoretical upper limit of risk reduction for a polygenic trait achievable through embryo selection or polygenic editing, and what are their key assumptions?
