# Research-Chain Note: BLSS Uncertainty Propagation Parameters & Distributions

## 1. Follow-up Questions

1. What Monte Carlo sample sizes and convergence criteria are used in NASA CELSS/MELiSSA BLSS uncertainty analyses?
2. Are crop harvest indices modeled as fixed species constants or as season/variety-dependent distributions, and how many growth cycles does empirical data span?
3. Do existing BLSS models treat water recovery rate and electrolyzer efficiency as independent random variables, or are they coupled through thermal/balance-of-plant models?
4. What sensitivity indices (Sobol, Morris) have been published for each parameter, and which parameters dominate total output variance?
5. How do published BLSS distributions change when the mission duration extends from 90-day transit to 2+ year Mars surface stays?
